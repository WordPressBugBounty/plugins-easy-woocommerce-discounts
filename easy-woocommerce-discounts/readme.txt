== 📦 WooCommerce Bulk Discount & Quantity Discount ==

A **WooCommerce bulk discount** (or **quantity discount**) automatically lowers the per-unit price as customers buy more — a proven way to increase average order value and clear inventory faster. Also called **tiered pricing**. Basic bulk pricing tiers are free and unlimited; advanced per-tier percentages and role-based tiers are in **PRO**.

- **Buy 1–5 units** → 0% off (regular price)
- **Buy 6–10 units** → 10% off
- **Buy 11–20 units** → 20% off
- **Buy 21+ units** → 30% off

**Volume kick discounts:** reward the *entire* order once a quantity threshold is hit — e.g. buy 10+, and all 10 units get 20% off retroactively, not just the units past the 10th. A distinct alternative to standard tiered pricing when you want an all-or-nothing volume incentive.

The redesigned **quantity pricing table** — with pre-built themes and a live settings preview — displays automatically on the product page, or anywhere via `[wccs_bulk_table]`.

== 🏷️ WooCommerce Category Discount ==

A **WooCommerce category discount** applies a percentage or fixed discount to an entire product category in one step — no editing individual products. Combine with date ranges or a minimum cart total, apply to multiple categories at once, and exclude specific products. Ideal for seasonal sales and clearance events.

- Set a **percentage discount** on any category (e.g. 20% off all "Winter Jackets")
- Set a **fixed amount discount** on any category (e.g. $10 off all "Accessories")

== 🛒 WooCommerce BOGO Deals — Buy One Get One Free ==

**BOGO deals** are one of the highest-converting promotions in e-commerce, and every common variation is free for simple and variable products. The free item adds to cart automatically, no manual step needed. Category-based and variation-based BOGO rules are in **PRO**.

- **Buy 1 Get 1 Free** — Classic buy one get one free
- **Buy 2 Get 1 Free** — Buy two, get the third at no cost
- **Buy 3 Get 1 Free** — Perfect for multi-pack promotions
- **Buy X Get Y** — Buy product A and receive product B free or at a discount
- **Buy One Get One 50% Off** — Buy one item, get the second at half price
- **Buy 2X Get 1Y Free** — cross-product reward, e.g. buy 2 shampoos, get 1 conditioner free
- **Buy 3, Cheapest Free** — automatically discounts the lowest-priced item in the cart

**BOGO use cases:**

- Cosmetics and personal care — buy one shampoo, get conditioner free
- Food and grocery — buy 3, pay for 2 multi-pack deals
- Apparel — buy two shirts, get one free to clear end-of-season stock
- SaaS or digital products — buy one license, get a second at no charge

== 📊 WooCommerce Tiered Pricing (PRO) ==

**Tiered pricing** — also called **volume pricing** or **volume discounts** — gives progressively larger discounts as customers buy more. Basic quantity tiers are free; advanced per-tier percentages, role-based tiers, and attribute conditions are in **PRO**: e.g. 5% off at 5 units, 12% off at 10, 20%+5% wholesale off at 20+.

PRO tiered pricing example:

- Quantity 1–4: regular price
- Quantity 5–9: 5% off
- Quantity 10–19: 12% off
- Quantity 20+: 20% off — plus an additional 5% off for wholesale user role

== Installation ==

1. In your WordPress admin, go to **Plugins → Add New**.
2. Search for **"Discount Rules and Dynamic Pricing for WooCommerce"**.
3. Click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
4. Go to **Woo Pricing & Discounts** menu to create your first discount rule.

Or upload manually:

1. Upload the `easy-woocommerce-discounts` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Activate through **Plugins** in the WordPress admin.
3. Visit the [Documentation](https://www.asanaplugins.com/docs/discount/quick-start/installation-and-setup) for detailed setup instructions.

- WordPress 4.4.1 or greater

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I create a bulk discount in WooCommerce? =

Install the plugin, go to **Woo Pricing & Discounts → Pricing tab → Add from Template**, and select the **Bulk Savings: Buy More, Save %** template. Set your quantity ranges and discount amounts (percentage or fixed). The bulk pricing table will appear automatically on the product page. You can also use the `[wccs_bulk_table]` shortcode to display it anywhere. Available in the free version. Full tutorial: [WooCommerce Bulk Discounts](https://www.asanaplugins.com/docs/discount/bulk/product-bulk-discounts).

= How do I create a BOGO (buy one get one free) deal in WooCommerce? =

Go to **Woo Pricing & Discounts → Pricing tab → Add from Template** and select one of the "BOGO: Buy 1 Get 1 Free" or "Free Gift: Buy X, Get Y for Free" templates. Select your buy and get products. The free product is added to the cart automatically. Buy 1 Get 1, Buy 2 Get 1, Buy 3 Get 1, and Buy X Get Y are all available in the free version. Full tutorial: [BOGO Deals in WooCommerce](https://www.asanaplugins.com/docs/discount/bogo/buy-1-get-1-free).

= How do I create a buy 2 get 1 free deal in WooCommerce? =

Go to **Woo Pricing & Discounts → Pricing tab → Add from Template** and select the "Buy 2 Get 1 Free (Same Item)" or "Combo Pack: Buy 2 of X, Get 1 Y Free" template. Select your buy and get products. The free item is added to the cart automatically. Available in the free version. Full tutorial: [Buy 2 Get 1 Free in WooCommerce](https://www.asanaplugins.com/docs/discount/bogo/buy-2-get-1-free).

= How do I create a category discount in WooCommerce? =

Go to **Woo Pricing & Discounts → Pricing tab → Add from Template** and select the "Category Discovery" template. Choose **Percentage** or **Price** for the Adjustment Type, then under **Discounted Products** select **Categories**. The discount applies to all products in those categories automatically. Available in the free version. Full tutorial: [WooCommerce Category Discounts](https://www.asanaplugins.com/docs/discount/simple/product-sale-pricing).

= How do I create a tiered pricing rule in WooCommerce? =

Basic quantity-based tiered discounts (buy more, save more) are available in the free version — go to **Woo Pricing & Discounts → Pricing tab → Add from Template** and select the **Bulk Savings: Buy More, Save %** template. Add multiple quantity ranges with different discount percentages. Advanced tiered pricing with role-based conditions, attribute targeting, and live price display is available in the **PRO version**. Full tutorial: [Tiered Discounts in WooCommerce](https://www.asanaplugins.com/docs/discount/tiered/product-tiered-pricing).

= What is the difference between bulk discount and tiered pricing? =

They refer to the same concept. "Bulk discount" and "quantity discount" mean giving a lower price when customers buy more items. "Tiered pricing" and "volume pricing" describe the same strategy with multiple price levels at different quantity thresholds. Basic bulk/quantity discounts are available in the free version. Advanced tiered pricing rules with additional conditions are available in PRO.

= What is a volume kick discount? =

Unlike standard tiered pricing, where only the units past a threshold get the lower price, a [volume kick discount](https://www.asanaplugins.com/docs/discount/bulk/volume-kick-discounts) applies the discount **retroactively to every unit** once the threshold is met — e.g. buy 10+, and all 10 units get 20% off, not just units 10 and up. Available in the free version.

= How do I set up a WooCommerce coupon that applies automatically? =

Create a **Cart Discount** rule and set your conditions (date, cart total, products, etc.). When a customer meets those conditions, the WooCommerce coupon discount is applied at checkout automatically — no code needed. You can also create **manual coupons** that require a specific code. Both modes are available in the free version.

= How do I create a discount or auto-apply coupon based on cart total? =

Go to **Woo Pricing & Discounts → Discounts tab → Add from Template** and select the **Minimum Spend** template. Set the cart total threshold and discount amount — for example, spend over $100, get 15% off. Available in the free version.

= How do I create a WooCommerce checkout fee? =

WooCommerce checkout fees are available in the **PRO version**. Go to **Woo Pricing & Discounts → Fees tab** to create a checkout fee rule. You can base fees on payment method, cart weight, shipping postcode, or specific products in the cart. Full tutorial: [Create Payment Gateway Fees in WooCommerce](https://www.asanaplugins.com/docs/discount/fees/payment-method-fees).

= Can I add a surcharge for a specific payment method in WooCommerce? =

Yes, this is a **PRO version** feature. Create a **Checkout Fee** rule and add a **Payment Method** condition — for example, add a 2% surcharge for credit card payments. Full tutorial: [Payment Method Fee in WooCommerce](https://www.asanaplugins.com/docs/discount/fees/payment-method-fees).

= Can I offer wholesale pricing or different prices per user role? =

This is a **PRO version** feature. With PRO, create a pricing rule and add a **User Role** condition. For example, wholesale customers get 25% off all products automatically when they log in. Full tutorial: [User Role Based Pricing for WooCommerce](https://www.asanaplugins.com/docs/discount/simple/role-based-pricing).

= Can I create a flash sale or scheduled discount? =

Yes, available in the free version. Every rule has a **Date Range** option (From Date / To Date). Set the start and end date and the rule activates and deactivates automatically. You can also target specific days of the week. The **PRO version** adds a **Countdown Timer** on product pages. Full tutorial: [WooCommerce Flash Sale](https://www.asanaplugins.com/woocommerce-flash-sale/).

Yes, available in the free version. When you create a bulk pricing rule, a quantity table appears on the product page automatically. Customize its position and style in the plugin settings. Use `[wccs_bulk_table]` shortcode to place it anywhere.
